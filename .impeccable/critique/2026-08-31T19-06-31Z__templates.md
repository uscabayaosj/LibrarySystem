## Design Specificity Verdict

The borrower half is authored. The .slip — perforated indigo stub stamped DUE/03/SEP in Fraunces — is a real object that restamps coral when overdue and apricot when due-soon. ISBNs in tabular mono; covers hue-clamped to an aqua→indigo arc; tab bar as the rail laid on its side.

The admin shell gets none of it. .slip appears in four member templates and zero admin ones. Four stat tiles, five data tables, a <details> form — rename the columns and it ships as an inventory manager. /admin/borrowing-history is the most generic screen on the surface: eight columns, no search, sorted by borrow date so the 26-days-late item sorts below the 7-days-late one under the Overdue filter.

The failures elsewhere are imports, not blandness: the sheet's macOS 12vh top-drop puts Confirm/Cancel at y≈267–311 on an 812px phone; the phone large-title crossfade holds .toolbar-heading at opacity 0 so "2 items need your attention" is invisible when the app opens.

The deepest gap is behavioural: the Explain-the-Block Rule is implemented on renewal and not on borrowing, and inverted on both deletes.

**Deterministic scan.** The detector as invoked returned exit 0 / 0 findings and that result is worthless — it emitted "DEGRADED - HTML parser modules unavailable (htmlparser2, css-select, css-tree, domutils)... findings are an undercount, not a clean bill of health". Zero true positives from the CLI detector.

**Visual overlays.** Injection succeeded; overlay ran in-page on 5 pages; live-server stopped and verified; git status clean. Load-bearing findings: low-contrast on every page, tiny-text on the 11px .row-note carrying every block explanation, flat-type-hierarchy on /admin/books, side-tab on the stat tile rule. ## Overall Impression

A product built with real taste, sitting on a fault line. One wrong comparison operator repeated in eight places makes the single number the product exists to get right disagree with itself, on both shells and on the phone's home-screen badge. models.py:180 documents precisely why timestamp comparison is wrong; eight other call sites do it anyway. Biggest opportunity: make the behaviour as principled as the pixels. Two rules already exist in the codebase — one canonical due-state, and explain the block at the control. Neither needs inventing; both need finishing.

## What's Working

1. **The renewal block explanation.** models.py:246 exposes renew_blocked_reason separately from can_renew so the UI can print the sentence; history.html:66 records why it is visible text rather than a title tooltip ("tooltips never fire on touch"). The reason derives from the same canonical model as the badge colour, so it cannot drift.
2. **The confirmation sheet is properly engineered.** base.html:252 + app.js:213: trigger stashed and restored, Cancel takes initial focus, Tab trapped, Escape closes, confirm self-disables with bfcache and 12s timeout recovery.
3. **Dark appearance is a second deliberate palette.** Rail stays solid #292168; window is indigo to near-black; separators flip rather than being reused. Thirteen screens walked in both appearances, no washed-out pairing.
4. **The reservation copy refuses manufactured urgency.** A lapsing hold never goes coral because it is not the member's fault.

## Priority Issues

### [P0] The overdue count and the overdue badge run on two different clocks
models.py:196 derives days_until_due from calendar dates in library-local time; models.py:177, models.py:128, routes/member.py:59, :161, routes/admin.py:57, :256, :336 all test due_date < datetime.utcnow(). A book due today at 14:00 Manila is 06:00 UTC. Live: alert "2 overdue books", tile Overdue 2 coral, toolbar "2 items need your attention" — and the alert's own line reads "was due Sep 01, 2026 (Due today)" with an apricot slip and a live Renew button. Admin shows the same split (tile 5, list badges 4). Violates PRODUCT.md Principle 1 by name. Fix: one local-midnight boundary helper, all eight call sites through it, plus a clock-fixed regression test.
Suggested command: $impeccable harden

### [P1] Blocked users are invited to act, made to confirm, then refused — four paths
search.html:97 renders Borrow on availability alone (17 live buttons for a blocked member, 13 at the loan cap), with a sheet promising a 14-day due date. member_detail.html:183, members.html:66, books.html:139: deletes always enabled, danger sheet asserts the deletion will happen, server then refuses — on a page displaying "5 On loan" 40px above the button. Inverts the product's own Explain-the-Block Rule and devalues every genuine destructive confirmation. Fix: borrow_blocked_reason beside renew_blocked_reason rendered as .row-note naming the number; disable deletes with the reason beside them and skip the sheet.
Suggested command: $impeccable clarify

### [P1] A closed dialog and a closed menu stay in every page's accessibility tree
app.css:1465 and app.css:576 hide with opacity:0; pointer-events:none only. Closed sheet measures visibility:visible, inert:false, aria-hidden:null, role:dialog, aria-modal:true, and its Cancel button is focusable. Every page including public login ends its tab order with four invisible menu items (incl. Sign Out) and two invisible dialog buttons with focus rings at opacity 0. aria-modal on a reachable node can confine a reader inside "Are you sure?". Fix: visibility:hidden with delayed transition plus inert from the handlers; give role="menu" its keyboard contract or drop the role.
Suggested command: $impeccable audit
